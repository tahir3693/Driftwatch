# Product

**Name:** Driftwatch
**Tagline:** "Catch the drift before it becomes a problem."

Driftwatch is an offline-first early-warning health copilot for astronauts on long missions. It is built for the NASA Space Apps challenge on health monitoring software for astronauts.

## What it does
- Builds a personal baseline for each astronaut.
- Raises explainable alerts with one of three statuses: Normal, Watch or Act.

## Boundaries
- Driftwatch is decision support, not a medical device.
- All demo data is simulated. The UI must label it "Simulated" wherever it is shown.
